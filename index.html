FAQ
Is this the e-Beach Wagon?

No. The autonomous follow-me beach cart in the viral TikTok videos is a one-of-a-kind machine designed and hand-built by KMJ Dynamics. It drives itself — a wearable UWB tag and four corner receivers track the operator so the cart follows hands-free, while 360° lidar steers it around obstacles. e-Beach Wagon is an unrelated company whose electric wagons are hand-throttle driven: the operator steers and controls speed with a thumb throttle, and as of August 2026 they do not offer a self-driving or follow-me product. If a search result pointed you to a store-bought electric wagon, that is not the cart from the video.

Where can I buy one?

The self-driving cart is not for sale — there is one, in the shop. The remote-control version has a waitlist. Email hello@kmjdynamics.com with subject “RC Cart Waitlist”. Or build your own.

How much does the RC cart cost?

Pricing has not been published yet — the number will be announced when the first batch opens for deposits. Welded aluminum frame, two 3,000 W motors, a 1.3 kWh LiFePO4 battery, industrial 24V steering servo, 16.5-inch balloon tires. No deposits are being taken; the current list is a no-commitment head count.

Who built it?

I did — KMJ Dynamics is a one-person shop. The welded aluminum frame, the 24V power electronics, the carbon-fiber-nylon printed hubs, and every line of the autonomy software came off the same bench. The whole build is documented publicly with dated videos: early follow-me tests in July 2026, and the viral video that passed a million views in August 2026.

How does it follow you — no camera, no phone?

A small ultra-wideband radio tag on your wrist gets ranged by four receivers on the cart's corners, which pins your position many times a second — no camera, no phone, no GPS. The cart steers to keep tracking behind you, while a spinning 360° lidar watches for people and obstacles and stops it at curbs and drop-offs. Prefer to drive? A switch on the remote hands over full manual control any time.

Can you drive it with the remote?

Yes. Electric wagons you can buy today keep the operator on a handle. This cart is driven from a handheld transmitter — servo steering, dual motors, you stand apart from it. That same architecture is what lets the follow-me brain drive it.
