Store-bought electric beach wagons keep you on the handle: the motor helps, you steer by muscle. This one is remote-controlled. You walk free. The cart goes where you point it.

The design is patent pending — U.S. provisional filed August 2026. The full self-driving cart from the videos is not for sale. This is the version I can build now.
FrameWelded 6061 aluminum, 1.5" square tube, 32" × 48" deck. Built 2022. The frame has never changed.
Weight160 lb total. Complete cart, no cargo.
Payload400 lb. Demonstrated with five roof-shingle bundles that weigh 80 lb each.
Speed~4.5 mph. Reverse about half that.
DriveTwo 3,000 W motors, one per rear wheel. #40 chain, 10:1 two-stage jackshaft — designed to climb steep inclines.
SteeringTwo kingpin pivot points on a fixed U-shaped steel support. 24V industrial servo. No front axle.
Wheels16.5" PU balloon tires on two-piece printed hubs, tire bonded. 2–4 psi; 2.5 psi for soft sand.
Battery24V 50Ah LiFePO4, under the frame.
ControlHandheld RC transmitter. No Pi. No follow-me. No lidar.

FAQ
Is this the e-Beach Wagon?

No. The cart in the videos is this one — I designed and built it. e-Beach Wagon is a different company. Their wagons are electric, but you stay on the handle with a thumb throttle. Nothing they sell follows you or drives from a remote.

Where can I buy one?

The self-driving cart is not for sale — there is one, in the shop. The remote-control version has a waitlist. Email hello@kmjdynamics.com with subject “RC Cart Waitlist”. Or build your own.

Can you drive it with the remote?

Yes. Electric wagons you can buy today keep the operator on a handle. This cart is driven from a handheld transmitter — servo steering, dual motors, you stand apart from it.
